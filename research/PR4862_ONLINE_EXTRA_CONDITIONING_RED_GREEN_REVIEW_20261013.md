# Independent LeRobot #4862 review: training succeeds but inherited online extra-conditioning loses its input

**2026-10-13 | High-confidence upstream bug finding, NOT merged.**

**Original upstream contribution:** [huggingface/lerobot #4862](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot/pull/4862), written by its own original author as part of a DP3 series. This forked diagnostic and prototype fix do **not** claim authorship of their proposed Diffusion Policy extension, nor external maintainer approval. AI assistance was used in the independent review/test and candidate-fix authoring; human understanding and review are required by [LeRobot's AI_POLICY.md](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md) before communicating or proposing this upstream.

## Reproducible causal chain

1. **Failure:** upstream #4862 introduces hooks `DiffusionPolicy._make_diffusion_model`, `DiffusionModel._extra_global_cond_dim`, `_extra_global_cond_feats` and `_has_conditioning_input` so a subclass can add `observation.extra` to loss-conditioning and supposedly inherit online `select_action()`. Its tests cover loss and U-Net width but NOT an online queued observation step.
2. **Root cause:** `DiffusionPolicy.reset()` only creates deque histories for the built-in `observation.state`, action, images and env-state. `populate_queues()` intentionally IGNORES any other key. After one observation, `predict_action_chunk()` reconstructs its batch by filtering for `key in self._queues`; this drops the extra condition. The new subclass's `_extra_global_cond_feats` then raises `KeyError: 'observation.extra'`. This is not an invalid caller: the subclass's training `compute_loss` accepts and learns from exactly the same input.
3. **Independent original-head failure:** [RED Linux+Windows GitHub Actions](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/lerobot/actions/runs/38369273029), both environments complete dependency installation, original five #4862 tests pass, then **2 additional tests FAIL on each OS**: true online `select_action` raises the predicted `KeyError`, and inspecting `model._queues` confirms no extra-key deque. [Source-only independent reproduction](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/lerobot/blob/validation/pr4862-online-extra-conditioning-falsifier-20261013/tests/policies/test_diffusion_hooks_online_review_regression.py).
4. **Candidate targeted repair:** add one opt-in `DiffusionPolicy._extra_observation_queue_keys(self) -> tuple[str,...]` hook returning empty by default (zero impact on original DiffusionPolicy). In `reset` explicitly register only the subclass-declared feature keys, require they be `observation.*`, reject duplicates and action keys. The test's minimal `ExtraObsPolicy` subclass declares `('observation.extra',)`. This retains the newly advertised online extra feature across its n_obs_steps history without queuing unrelated metadata.
5. **Independent fix verification:** [GREEN Linux+Windows exact variant](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/lerobot/actions/runs/38369610806), each OS **7/7 passed**, including all five original PR-hook tests plus two new online contract regressions. [Prototype patch](https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/lerobot/compare/validation/pr4862-online-extra-conditioning-falsifier-20261013...validation/pr4862-online-extra-conditioning-fix-20261013) is deliberately confined to this fork and **NOT an authorized upstream PR**.

## Why this is technically consequential

Training / offline conditioning works while actual on-policy inference does not. A 3D vision or pointcloud-conditioned policy could pass all unit tests yet fail at the first real executed action chunk. This is a real representation provenance / execution-time interface inconsistency; general Diffusion Policy extensibility requires carrying extra conditioning through *both* modes, not just the U-Net input dimension.
